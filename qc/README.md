# Quality-control reports

These two documents are the output of [`data_quality.py`](../data_quality.py) and
[`data_quality_1min.py`](../data_quality_1min.py), committed **exactly as the scripts produced them**
(Spanish, like the rest of the working documents).

They are derived summaries — row counts, gap tables, defect rates, correlations, and a handful of
example bars — **not the datasets**. The underlying series are not redistributed here: Databento's and
Yahoo Finance's terms do not allow it. Download your own copy and regenerate these files; see
"Reproducing this" in the [main README](../README.md).

Nothing in these reports is corrected, filled or smoothed. Everything odd is reported as found —
including the defect that removed six years of intraday data from the search.
